How to play

  • BThe rock's colour tells you its column: B blue, I red, N grey, G green, O orange.
  • ✓On your card: tap it. Not on your card: let it fly. A wrong tap costs a heart.
  • 💣Bombs must be tapped before they fall back down.
  • ★Golden rocks mark your best cell. The gold-ringed number pays ×5.
  • ♥Every BINGO gives a heart back and lets you pick the next card: Mild ×1, Spicy ×2 or Inferno ×3.
