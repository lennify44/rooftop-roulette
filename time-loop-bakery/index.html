    How it works

    • 🔥 Tap an oven to bake. Tap it again when it dings to put the pastry on the counter. Too long and it burns.
    • 🙋 When a customer’s bubble glows, tap them to hand over what they want.
    • 📓 Everyone you meet goes into the notebook and onto the timeline at the top.
    • ⟲ Rewind any time. Nothing carries over except what you know.
    • ⭐ Serve everyone in one loop to finish the day: 3 loops or fewer is three stars.
