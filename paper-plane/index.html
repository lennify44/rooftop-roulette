How to play

Letters pile up in your inbox. Windows in the building across the street light up for a few seconds: that's who is waiting for mail. Tap at the height of a lit window to throw a paper plane towards it. While it flies, hold your thumb down to pull its nose up (it climbs and slows down) and let go to dive (it speeds up). Fly it into a lit window. A hit dead in the middle is PERFECT and scores double, gold express letters score triple, pink love letters score ×5 and give a heart back if you fly them into a window with a heart, and every delivery in a row raises your streak multiplier. A plane that hits the wall, a dark window, a pigeon or the street is lost. If more than 6 letters pile up, you lose a heart. Every 10 deliveries the next block gets windier and busier. New plane designs unlock as your best score grows.
