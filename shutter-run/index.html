How to shoot

  • 📷 Hold the left half to raise the camera (time slows while focus lasts), let go to take the photo.
  • 🎯 Points for a centred subject, its best moment (wings spread, firework at its peak) and for shooting in mid-air.
  • 🦘 Right half jumps, hold for higher. Jumping also lifts the viewfinder to high subjects.
  • 🧺 Don’t jump into washing lines, jump over skylights. Bumps cost film. Out of film? Run to the newsroom roof.
