How to play

Drag anywhere and your magnet core follows your thumb. Every piece of scrap it touches sticks to it, and the blob grows. Robots that touch it get stuck and start chewing pieces off (and a bomber that sticks blows up after two seconds). Double-tap (or press Space) to BLAST: every stuck piece flies outward and wrecks the first robot it hits. The more robots one blast takes out, the more each one is worth. But a bigger blob is slower, and every bullet that hits it knocks a piece off. If the core itself is hit, you lose a heart. Smashed robots drop new scrap. Between waves you pick an upgrade; every fifth wave brings a boss. New core colours unlock as your best score grows. The daily challenge gives everyone the same robots, scrap and upgrade offers for the day, with its own leaderboard.
