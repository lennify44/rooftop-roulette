How to run

  • 👆 Right side: jump. Hold for a higher, longer jump.
  • 🤸 Left side: roll. Tap it as you land after a big drop and the cargo feels nothing. Rolling also ducks under pipes and washing lines.
  • 📦 Hard landings, bumps and pigeons crack the cargo. Falling between roofs loses it.
  • ⭐ Deliver it: one star. Two and three stars need more of the cargo intact (each route says how much). Faster = bigger tips.
  • 🔓 Stars unlock new routes and trickier cargo.
