The plates on the belt carry playing cards. Your customers order poker hands. Grab the right plates, serve the hand before they lose patience, and don’t waste money: every plate costs its price.

All games

How it works

  • Tap a plate to take its card into your tray. You pay the plate’s price: white $2, blue $3, red $4, black $5.
  • Build the hand in a customer’s bubble, then tap Serve. A better hand than ordered pays more.
  • Tap a card in your tray to throw it away. Wasabi cards stick: they pay double but can’t be thrown away.
  • Golden plates are wild. Mystery plates are cheap and face down.
  • Three angry customers close the bar early.
