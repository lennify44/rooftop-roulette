A rooftop relay with your own ghosts. Your runner never stops running unless you hold them back. Doors need someone on the plate, walls need someone to climb on. Run a leg, then team up with its ghost.

All games

How it works

  • 🏃 The runner runs by themselves. Hold the left half to stop, tap the right half to jump (hold for higher).
  • 👻 Press Next leg and your run becomes a ghost that repeats it exactly, and keeps standing where you left it.
  • 🟦 Ghosts hold plates, press buttons and flip levers. A ghost standing still is a step you can climb on.
  • ⭐ Stars: reach the flag, use no more legs than par, beat the par time.
