How to climb

  • 👆 Tap the left half to leap to the left wall, the right half for the right wall. Each move climbs one floor; tapping the wall you’re on hops straight up.
  • ⚠️ A red marker at the top shows where something will fall. Move out of that lane.
  • 🕳️ Missing beams make you slide down to the next one. Rusty beams crack if you stay. Glass is slippery.
  • 🪝 Tap the side a crane hook is on as it swings past to ride it up.
  • 🌪️ Keep ahead of the dust storm.
