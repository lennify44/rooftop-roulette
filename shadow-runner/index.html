How to run

  • ☀️ Sunlight makes you smoke. Shade heals you. Full smoke and you’re a bat.
  • 👆 Right side: jump (hold for higher). Left side: hold to brake and wait in a shadow.
  • 🚚 Trucks and clouds carry their shadow with them: brake to stay under it.
  • 🚪 Run past a glowing door in the shade to deliver a parcel.
  • ☂️ Umbrellas shield you for a few seconds. Garlic stands and open cellars trip you.
  • 🕛 Reach noon to finish the district. Deliver all 10 parcels for a second star, and keep your smoke under 70% for the third.
