    Controls

    Right side
    Jump. Hold longer to jump higher.
    Left side
    Slide under pipes and birds. In the air: drop fast, keep holding to roll.
    Walls
    Grab the ledge, tap jump to climb. Hold jump when you hit it to wall-run.
    Obstacles
    Crash through vents, nets or bins: +20% speed each, up to +60%. Pipes still knock you out.
    Action
    Red barrels blast you up and forward. Cracked roofs collapse. Jump up to grab zip lines. Jetpack: hold jump to fly.
    Keyboard
    Space or ↑ jump · ↓ slide · B bet · P pause
