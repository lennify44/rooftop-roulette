How it works
Start
Every ride starts with a $100 pot.
Each floor
Win the mini-game and the pot grows (×1.4, a bit more on every floor). Every 5th floor is a VIP floor: harder, ×2.5.
Twists
The second dial can add Double pay, Hot seat, Tip jar, Slow-mo, Free pass or a rare Jackpot floor.
Snap risk
From floor 3 on, every ride up can snap the cable. The risk grows each floor and is shown on the button.
Express
Skip three floors without games, but the cable snaps one time in three (one in six with cable grease).
Bust
Lose a game or snap the cable and the pot is gone. Your bank stays safe.
